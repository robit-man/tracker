Room behavior
The room replicates only the nested catalog and metadata. File bytes stay on peers who possess them. GET and STREAM pull verified chunks across all available seeders in parallel. STREAM is ephemeral and does not make the viewer a seeder. Password-locked files expose only scrambled catalog names and ciphertext until locally unlocked. There is no application file-size cap; practical limits are browser, filesystem, codec, and device resources.
